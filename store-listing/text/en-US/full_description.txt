SMS Toolbox backs up your text messages to your own Gmail account, forwards the messages you choose to another phone number, and sends your own messages on a schedule — with no account to create and no server of ours in between.

WHY SMS TOOLBOX?
Your text messages live in one place: your phone. Lose it, break it, or switch devices and years of conversations go with it. SMS Toolbox copies them into your own Gmail, where they are searchable, safe, and yours. It also covers a work phone, monitors a second SIM, forwards one-time codes to a shared family number, or makes sure a message goes out while you are busy.

BACKUP — Your messages, safe in your own Gmail
• Backs up your SMS to a Gmail account you own, using an app password you create
• Choose the Gmail label they are filed under (default: "SMS")
• Customise the email subject, with placeholders for the contact name and number
• Each contact's messages are grouped into a single Gmail conversation
• Original message dates are preserved, so your history reads correctly
• Back up everything you already have, or just messages from now on
• Picks up where it left off if interrupted, and never uploads the same message twice
• Your messages go straight from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection — we never see them

REDIRECT — Forward messages by rule
• Create simple rules: match a sender (by text or contact) and pick a target number
• Works for standard SMS messages
• First matching rule wins — drag to reorder your rules
• Optional custom prefix added to every forwarded message (e.g. "SMS Toolbox:")
• Choose whether forwarding is allowed while roaming
• History of the last 50 forwarded messages, with sender, target, and matched rule
• Get a notification every time a message is forwarded

SCHEDULE — Send texts automatically, later
• Compose a message once, pick a contact, a date, and a time — the app sends it for you
• Repeat daily, on work-week days, bi-weekly, monthly, or yearly, with an optional stop date
• Organize scheduled messages into categories
• History of every scheduled message that was sent

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account with us, no login, no backend server — we run nothing that could receive your data
• Rules, schedules, contacts, and history are stored only on your device
• Backup goes directly from your phone to YOUR Gmail account over an encrypted connection. It is off until you turn it on, and we never see a single message
• Your Gmail app password is encrypted on your device and sent only to Google. You can revoke it at any time from your Google account
• Forwarded and scheduled messages are sent directly through your own carrier's SMS network — never through a server of ours, because we don't have one
• Only anonymous crash and usage data is collected to help fix bugs — never phone numbers, contact names, or message content
• The full source code is public, so every claim here can be verified: github.com/neteinstein/SMS-Redirect

ALSO INCLUDES
• Contact picker for senders and targets
• Drag-and-drop reordering and category grouping for both rules and schedules
• Light & dark mode with Material You dynamic color
• Export and import your rules and schedules as a settings file, anytime
• Choose which tab opens first — Backup, Redirect, or Schedule — to match how you use the app
• Available in English and Portuguese

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
SMS Toolbox only asks for what it needs: SMS permissions (to back up, detect and send messages), Contacts (to pick numbers by name), Phone State (to detect roaming), and Internet (to reach Gmail when backup is on). See exactly how each permission is used in the in-app Privacy Policy and in the public source code.
