SMS Toolbox is a messaging app for your text messages. Set it as your default SMS app to send and receive SMS and MMS, and get a toolbox around them: back up to your own Gmail, restore on a new phone, redirect and schedule. No account to create, and no server of ours in between.

SEND AND RECEIVE SMS AND MMS
• All your conversations in one list, newest first, with contact names and unread markers
• Send and receive text messages, picture messages (MMS) and group messages
• Attach photos, videos, audio or contact cards; large photos are resized to fit
• Notifications with quick reply and "mark as read", hidden on the lock screen
• Open "send message" links and share text or pictures from other apps
• Reply by text from the incoming-call screen
• Failed messages are marked, and can be sent again with a tap

REDIRECT
• Forward incoming SMS that match your rules (by sender or number) to another phone
• Rules in the order you choose, optional prefix, and a forward history

SCHEDULE
• Write a message once and have it sent later: once, daily, on work days, every two weeks, monthly or yearly

BACKUP TO YOUR OWN GMAIL
• Backs up every SMS you receive and send, automatically, under a Gmail label you choose
• Each contact gets a single Gmail conversation, and original dates are kept
• Back up your whole history, or only messages from now on
• Goes straight from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection

RESTORE TO ANY PHONE
• Brings your backed-up texts back from Gmail, received and sent, each in the right conversation
• Skips messages already on the phone, so it is safe to run again

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your messages stay on your phone. No account, no login, and no server of ours
• Gmail backup is off until you turn it on. It uses an app password you create, stored encrypted with the Android Keystore and sent only to Google
• Crash reports and usage statistics (Firebase) never include phone numbers, contact names or message text
• Fully open source: github.com/neteinstein/SMS-Redirect

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
SMS Toolbox first asks to become your default SMS app; Android then gives it access to your SMS and MMS, which it needs to show, send and receive them. Prefer to keep your current messaging app? Redirect, Schedule and Backup still work with SMS permissions only; Messages and Restore stay disabled until SMS Toolbox is your default SMS app. Contacts: show names instead of numbers. Notifications: new messages, backup and restore progress. Battery exemption: deliver, forward and send scheduled messages on time. Internet: reach Gmail when backup is on.
