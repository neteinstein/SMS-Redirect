Your texts live on one phone. SMS Toolbox keeps them safe in your own Gmail, brings them back on a new phone, forwards the ones that matter, and sends messages for you on schedule. No account to create, and no server of ours in between.

BACKUP TO YOUR OWN GMAIL
• Backs up every SMS you receive and every SMS you send, automatically, as it happens
• Files them under a Gmail label you choose (default: "SMS"), searchable like any email
• Each contact gets a single Gmail conversation, and original dates are kept
• Customise the email subject with the contact's name and number
• Back up your whole history, or only messages from now on
• Never uploads the same message twice, and picks up where it left off if interrupted
• Goes straight from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection

RESTORE TO ANY PHONE
• Brings your backed-up texts back from Gmail, received and sent, each in the right place
• Skips messages that are already on the phone, so it is safe to run again
• Android only lets the default SMS app add messages, so SMS Toolbox borrows that role while the restore runs, then reminds you to switch back

FORWARD WHAT MATTERS
• Simple rules: match a sender by contact, number or text, and choose where to send it
• Made for one-time codes, a second SIM, delivery alerts, or family
• First matching rule wins: drag to reorder, and group rules by category
• Optional prefix on forwarded messages, and your choice whether to forward while roaming
• History of the last 50 forwards, with the rule that matched
• Optional notification every time a message is forwarded

SCHEDULE MESSAGES
• Write a message once, pick a contact, a date and a time, and it is sent for you
• Repeat daily, on work-week days, bi-weekly, monthly or yearly, with an optional stop date
• Organise schedules by category, with a history of everything sent
• Sent from your own SIM, through your carrier

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no login, and no server of ours: nothing we run can receive your messages
• Rules, schedules and history stay on your phone
• Gmail backup is off until you turn it on. It uses an app password you create, stored encrypted with the Android Keystore and sent only to Google
• Crash reports and usage statistics (Firebase) never include phone numbers, contact names or message text
• Fully open source, so every claim here can be checked: github.com/neteinstein/SMS-Redirect

ALSO INCLUDED
• Material You design, in light and dark mode
• Export and import your rules and schedules as a file
• Choose which tab opens first: Backup, Redirect or Schedule
• In English and Portuguese

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
Each permission is asked for only when a feature needs it. SMS: back up, detect, forward and send messages. Contacts: pick people by name. Notifications: progress and confirmations. Battery exemption: on-time forwarding and scheduled sends. Internet: reach Gmail when backup is on. Default SMS app: only while a restore you started is running.
