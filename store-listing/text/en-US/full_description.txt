SMS Toolbox backs up your text messages to your own Gmail account — with no account to create and no server of ours in between.

WHY BACK UP YOUR SMS?
Your text messages live in one place: your phone. Lose it, break it, or switch devices and years of conversations go with it. SMS Toolbox copies them into your own Gmail, where they are searchable, safe, and yours — and stay available from any device you sign in to.

BACKUP — Your messages, safe in your own Gmail
• Backs up your SMS to a Gmail account you own, using an app password you create
• New messages are backed up automatically as they arrive
• Back up everything you already have, or just messages from now on
• Choose the Gmail label they are filed under (default: "SMS")
• Customise the email subject, with placeholders for the contact name and number
• Each contact's messages are grouped into a single Gmail conversation
• Original message dates are preserved, so your history reads correctly
• Picks up where it left off if interrupted, and never uploads the same message twice
• Your messages go straight from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection — we never see them

RESTORE — Get your messages back on your phone
• Restore your backed-up SMS from Gmail to a new or reset phone, with their original dates
• During a restore, Android asks you to make SMS Toolbox your default SMS app — the only way Android lets an app write messages. When it finishes, switch back to your usual messaging app
• Messages already on the phone are not duplicated
• Your backup also stays in Gmail, readable and searchable from any phone or computer
• Export the app's settings to a file and restore them on a new phone in seconds

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account with us, no login, no backend server — we run nothing that could receive your data
• Backup goes directly from your phone to YOUR Gmail account over an encrypted connection. It is off until you turn it on, and we never see a single message
• Your Gmail app password is encrypted on your device and sent only to Google. You can revoke it at any time from your Google account
• The app keeps no second copy of your messages — only a one-way fingerprint of each one, so it knows what is already backed up
• Only anonymous crash and usage data is collected to help fix bugs — never phone numbers, contact names, or message content
• The full source code is public, so every claim here can be verified: github.com/neteinstein/SMS-Redirect

ALSO INCLUDES
• Backup progress notification
• Light & dark mode with Material You dynamic color
• Available in English and Portuguese

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
SMS Toolbox only asks for what it needs: SMS access (to back up your messages, notice new ones, and write them back during a restore), Internet (to reach Gmail), and Notifications (to show backup progress). It never sends SMS, and is your default SMS app only while you restore. See exactly how each permission is used in the Privacy Policy and in the public source code.
